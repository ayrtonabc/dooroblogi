What is an oak trim batten

Oak trim battens — definition and specification

An oak trim batten is a finish board made of natural oak, cut to size to a given profile, length and section. It goes where the wood is seen up close: at floor level, on a wall, around a window and inside a sliding door frame.

The same material goes into 4–8 mm battens and into ready-made ceiling and wall panels.

About the trim battens

Wood you get to see up close

A trim batten goes exactly where the eye stops: at the floor, around a window, on a wall, in the transition between rooms. That is why we cut to size rather than to stock dimensions.

We work with natural oak. We do not stain it or make it look like another wood — we keep the figure, the knots and the colour the bark gave it. Two selections are available: knot-free oak, where the texture itself is the point, and oak with knots, where the figure becomes part of the interior.

The same material becomes ready-made ceiling and wall panels — our trim battens are the base for SEDO panels. If you need a specific profile or a ready length, write to us: we will quote it and give you a lead time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The six questions we get asked most — answered briefly and straight.

What is the difference between the knot-free and the knotty selection?

Same oak, same sizes — only the natural structure of the timber differs. The knot-free selection takes clean boards, the knotty selection keeps the boards with natural knots, which we do not remove. The price depends on the selection, so name it in your enquiry.

Do you sell only in wholesale quantities?

Not wholesale only — we work mainly with joiners, fit-out contractors and finishing companies, but we also serve private clients. Tell us the scale of your order and we will match the delivery format and the quote to it.

How do I get the price list?

We send the price list individually, because it depends on profile, length, thickness and quantity. Fill in the quote form or call us — we send the price list to the email you give, together with the lead time.

Do you supply timber cut to size?

Yes — that is the core of what we do. Give us the length, section and quantity and we will prepare elements ready for installation. For battens you also set the thickness: 4 mm or 8 mm.

Which finishes are available?

Natural, brushed and smoked. Smoking changes the colour of the wood, not its technical properties — hardness and geometry stay the same.

Where do you deliver and how long does it take?

We ship by courier across Poland, and quote delivery outside Poland individually. We state the lead time with the quote — usually a few working days from order confirmation.

Tell us what you want to build with wood

Dimensions, application, quantity — we reply with the price list and a lead time on the same day the enquiry arrives.
