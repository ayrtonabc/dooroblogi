FAQ

Frequently asked questions about oak trim battens

How much does an oak trim batten cost?

The price depends on length, section, profile and quantity, which is why we do not publish it. Send the dimensions in your enquiry — we reply with a written quote that states the price per sales unit and the lead time.

Which unit do you sell in?

Trim battens and oak battens are sold per linear metre, panels per square metre or per piece. The unit is stated in the quote.

Does the price depend on the wood selection?

Yes. Knot-free oak and oak with knots are two different selections, so we quote them separately — always the one your enquiry is about.

Is the quote binding?

Yes — the quote applies to the stated batch of timber and to the date we give. Once you accept it, we prepare the order.

How do I get a quote fastest?

We reply during working hours. The fastest way is the phone or Facebook — there you can also send the dimension you do not have on a list.

Can I get a sample profile?

Availability and shipping cost of a sample are confirmed in the quote — tell us which profile you need.
