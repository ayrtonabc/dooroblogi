Oak trim battens

Oak trim battens cut to size: length, section and profile matched to your project.

An oak trim batten is a finish element people see up close — at the floor, on the wall, around a window or beside a sliding door. That is why we cut to size rather than to stock dimensions.

We work with natural oak. Two selections are available: knot-free oak, where the grain itself is the point, and oak with knots, where the figure of the wood becomes part of the character of the room. Both share the same hardness and the same geometry — only the selection differs.

Length, section and profile are set from your enquiry. Send us the dimensions and we will reply with a written quote and a lead time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about oak trim battens

Do you sell oak trim battens in fixed lengths?

No. We cut to size — send the length, section and profile in your enquiry and we quote that exact piece.

Which wood selections do you offer?

Two, both from natural oak: knot-free (the grain alone is the point) and with knots (the figure of the wood becomes part of the room).

How much does an oak trim batten cost?

The price depends on length, section, profile and quantity, so we put it in a written quote — together with the lead time and delivery.
