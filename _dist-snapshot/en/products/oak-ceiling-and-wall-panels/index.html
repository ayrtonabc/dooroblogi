Product

Oak ceiling and wall panels

Ready-made panels from our oak trim battens — cladding and ceiling finishes in one wood selection.

This is the most practical way to work with our wood. Oak trim battens become ready-made ceiling and wall panels — with finished profiles and edges, prepared for installation.

We produce panels in different wood selections: from knot-free oak, which gives a calm and uniform interior, to oak with knots, which fills the wall and gives it character. The same material, a completely different room.

One of our customers is SEDO — their ready-made ceiling and wall panels are made from our trim battens. If you are looking for a specific system, write to us and we will tell you which of our profiles fits your project.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about oak trim battens

Are the panels ready for installation?

Yes. Profiles and edges are finished — nothing needs trimming on site.

Which variants do you produce?

In two wood selections (knot-free and with knots) and three finishes: natural, brushed and smoked.

Do you set the panel format to the project?

Yes. The module format and the fixing method are confirmed in the quote — tell us which system you are building.
