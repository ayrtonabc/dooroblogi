Delivery and payment

How we ship oak trim battens

Everything goes by courier. We match the packaging to the order and state the lead time in the quote — usually a few working days from dimension confirmation.

An ordinary order reaches you by courier within Poland. We match the packaging to length and quantity so the battens do not travel loose — larger quantities go on a pallet.

We confirm the lead time in the quote, once profile, length and quantity are agreed. As a rule it is a few working days from dimension confirmation.

Shipping outside Poland is possible, but we quote it individually — write to us where and how much timber, and we will give you the cost and the transit time.
