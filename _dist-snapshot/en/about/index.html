About us

Oak trim batten workshop in Słonecznik near Olsztyn

Doors Obłogi is a natural oak workshop in the Warmian-Masurian region. We produce trim battens, oak battens and ready-made panels — and deliver them to joiners, contractors and people building their own homes.

The company name says it plainly: trim battens are oak finish battens. We do not make furniture and we are not a finishing brand — we make the wood other people finish with.

We work with natural oak: no staining, no toning, no pretending to be a species we do not have. Every piece comes from one block of timber, so the figure on every leaf is different — and that is the point.

For a joiner what matters is repeatability: the same profile, the same selection, the next batch delivered on time. For a private client what matters is wood you can look at closely and that will still be there in five years.

Principles

Three things we do not change

01

The wood stays natural

We do not stain and we do not tone. A knot stays a knot, and pale oak does not pretend to be brown oak.

02

Size is agreed before cutting

We do not ship elements "roughly" — there is nothing to trim on site.

03

The date is agreed before shipping

We say when the wood will arrive, and it arrives then. We do not move a date without telling you.
